Case 00 · Monthly Cash Forecast

A forecast that fills itself — and knows when to ask

Every month, each leader of business unit predicts when every open invoice will be paid — this month, next month or the one after — inside one shared, live spreadsheet that feeds an eight-figure cash forecast. I rebuilt my part of it as a multi-agent system: it reads payments, terms and customer emails, proposes every cell with a reason, and writes only after I approve. My job moved from filling >3,700 invoice rows to reviewing the accounts where my judgment changes the answer.

The problem I found first

I hit the collection target every month, but missed the accuracy goal in 7 of 9 months. I was collecting ~113% of what I forecast. It felt safe, and it was wrong.

The real goal

Forecasting high can cost most of the payout; forecasting low only costs the bonus. So the target isn't "perfect", it's 100–104%.

The math underneath

Two classic ideas: survival analysis for timing, the newsvendor problem for the total

When will it pay?

Survival analysis, as in credit risk: each customer's history gives the odds an open invoice pays in each month. It goes to the likeliest one.

How much to commit?

When overshooting costs more than undershooting, the best bet sits below the average, as in the newsvendor problem. A 5% cushion moves the least likely invoices out first.

How I build

Four steps that repeat

01
Map the manual process first. How the job is done today, decision by decision, with no AI.
02
Decide where AI belongs. "Copy A into B" is plain code. "Read, classify, decide" is where a model earns its place.
03
Build the human gate in from the start. Approval before anything reaches a customer, from day one.
04
Turn every correction into a written rule — so the tools improve with use instead of decaying.

Three of the six outgrew a single procedure. Once a tool needs to remember last month, it becomes a system: one stage writes a ledger another reads.

Governance

Where the AI has to stop

✋
Nothing customer-facing sends itself. Every email, invoice and ticket waits for a human.
🚫
Never chase a credit balance. Net-negative accounts are excluded regardless of status.
✍️
Never invent commentary. A new account shows an empty cell. Blank is the honest answer.
🙋
Never guess a classification. Unknown owner means the run pauses and asks me.
📉
Never report a number it can't prove. A failed reconciliation aborts the report.

"A tool that stops and asks is worth more than one that's confidently wrong."
